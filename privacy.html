Privacy policy

MonBus PdM app — last updated: 20 November 2026

MonBus PdM is a free, independent app showing timetables, journeys and traffic news for the évolitY bus network (Pays de Montbéliard, France). It is published by an independent developer, reachable at MonBus_dev@outlook.fr, who is the controller of the processing described here.

The app asks for no account, name, email address or phone number.

1. Data processed and why

Location (optional)

With your permission, the app reads the phone's location to show stops and incidents near you. The location is used only on the phone: it is neither sent to our servers nor stored. You can revoke the permission at any time in the phone settings.

Notifications

  • Departure reminders are scheduled on the phone itself; nothing is sent.
  • Network news (diversions, strikes): if you allow notifications, the phone receives a technical notification identifier (“push token”), stored on our server so we can send you these alerts. It does not identify you. It is deleted once the app is uninstalled (as soon as the notification service reports it).

Crowding feedback and incident reports (optional)

When you rate how busy a bus is or report an incident, the app sends: the line, direction, stop, time, your feedback, a random identifier created on the phone (only used to count distinct people) and, if you chose one, your nickname, visible to other riders. This is used to warn other riders and to build crowding statistics. It is kept for at most 12 months.

Advertising

The app is funded by Google AdMob advertising. On first launch in Europe, a Google consent form lets you accept or refuse personalised advertising; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → My ad choices. Google may process the phone's advertising ID, IP address and technical information to serve, cap and measure ads. See how Google uses this data.

Local partners

The app may feature shops and businesses of the Pays de Montbéliard (map markers, announcements). Announcements sent as notifications only go to people who explicitly opted in within the app, and can be turned off at any time. No data about you is shared with partners.

Data kept on the phone

Your favourites, reminders, language, theme and preferences are stored only on the phone and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Contact

If you write to us, your email address and message are only used to reply to you.

2. Legal bases

  • Your consent: location, notifications, personalised advertising, partner notifications.
  • Our legitimate interest in running the app: feedback and reports, non-personalised advertising, security.

3. Service providers

We do not sell any data. Data is processed on our behalf by:

  • Supabase: database hosting (push tokens, feedback, network news);
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: notification delivery;
  • Google AdMob: advertising;
  • GitHub: automatic collection of traffic news published on evolity.fr.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union, under the safeguards provided by the GDPR (European Commission standard contractual clauses or the Data Privacy Framework).

4. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to and restrict processing. As the app does not know who you are, please tell us your nickname or the date of your report if possible. Write to MonBus_dev@outlook.fr: we reply within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

5. Children

The app is meant for all riders of the network. It does not knowingly collect data that identifies a child.

6. Changes

This policy may change along with the app. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
